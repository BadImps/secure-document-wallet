My Vault 2.0 adds two private vault areas:

- Personal Information: add and edit encrypted information, extract text from scanned documents or notes with on-device OCR, copy entries, and manage them in a WhatsApp-style list.
- Media: import encrypted photos, videos, and voice notes into private app storage that is not added to Gallery or Photos.
- IDs and bank cards now support press-and-hold landscape viewing at natural proportions.
- Back navigation from all four document categories now returns directly to the My Vault homepage while the secure session remains active.

Validation: clean Android build, unit tests, and Android lint all pass.
