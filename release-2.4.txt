My Vault 2.4 keeps the Documents stack focused on a first-page preview; tapping the preview opens the complete document. New PDF and Word DOCX uploads now retain an encrypted copy of the original file. Exporting a newly uploaded DOCX returns that original byte-for-byte so its styles, fonts, spacing, tables, headers, and page layout are not rebuilt by My Vault. The receiving device still needs the fonts used by the document to display them identically.
