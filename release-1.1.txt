My Vault 1.1

- Renamed the Android app and PC portal to My Vault.
- Added automatic update checks after unlocking the vault.
- Added Check For Updates to My Vault Settings.
- Downloads are verified with SHA-256 before Android is allowed to open the installer.
- Added the Android network and update-install permissions required for cloud access and in-app updates.

Your existing encrypted documents and PIN remain in place when updating from version 1.0.
