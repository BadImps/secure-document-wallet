My Vault 2.2 refreshes the visual experience and card controls:

- A lively new homepage with a teal hero header, six colorful gradient vault spaces, brighter icon wells, and a gold scan action.
- Flick down requires less movement and uses much faster outgoing and incoming animations.
- Image uploads open Samsung Gallery directly on Samsung devices, with the standard Android image picker as a fallback.
- The landscape ID and bank-card viewer now flips between front and back when tapped.
- Enlarge mode includes a clear X close control; tapping the card no longer exits the viewer.

Validation: clean Android build, unit tests, and Android lint all pass.
