My Vault 1.4

- Renamed the homepage action to Scan.
- A quick tap starts the normal automatic scanner.
- Press and hold Scan for Document, Certificate, and Card profiles.
- Document scans are prepared in an 8 × 11 portrait frame.
- Certificate scans are prepared in an 11 × 8 landscape frame.
- Card scans use a rounded bank-card frame.
- Card capture guides you through the front first and the back second.
- After both card sides are captured, choose Bank Cards or ID / Permit before encrypting and saving.

ML Kit automatic edge detection, cleanup, encryption, cloud backup, export, and card flipping remain available.
