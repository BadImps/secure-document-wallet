My Vault 3.11 improves bank-card expiry OCR.

- Uses Good Thru, Valid Thru, Valid Until, Expiry, or Expires when a label is readable.
- If no label is found, searches the entire front card for a standalone expiry date.
- Prioritizes MM/YY, MM-YY, MM.YY, and four-digit-year variants.
- Can also fall back to full numeric or written-month dates.
- When several dates appear, prefers the most likely future expiry instead of an older Member Since date.
