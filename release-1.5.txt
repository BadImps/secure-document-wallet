My Vault 1.5

- Replaced the homepage Scan text button with a centered circular scan badge.
- Quick tap and press-and-hold scanning behavior remain available.
- Added layered stack artwork to every homepage category tile.
- Bank Cards now displays an almost-invisible wallet stack behind the active card.
- Tap a card to flip between front and back.
- Flick downward to rotate to the next saved card.
- Changed new vault PINs to four digits.
- After biometric confirmation, entering the fourth digit unlocks My Vault automatically.
- Removed the Unlock and Close App buttons from PIN entry.
- Existing six-digit vaults receive a one-time, rollback-safe migration that re-encrypts every vault file under the new four-digit PIN.
- Updated the PC portal to accept the new four-digit PIN.

Encrypted documents, scanning profiles, exports, cloud backup, and OTA updates remain intact.
